## Single Sign On (SSO)

In both Community and Enterprise Editions of Onyx, you can configure basic authentication with email and password.

Additionally, you can configure SSO through popular identity providers such as Google, Okta, and Azure EntraID (OAuth,
OIDC, and SAML are all supported).

## Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

RBAC protection to documents and resources are only available in the Enterprise Edition of Onyx.
The following resources can be gated by users and groups.
- Agents
- Actions
- Document access
- Usage rate limits

## Document Access

Different access to documents is only available in the Enterprise Edition of Onyx.
The two ways of gating access to documents are:

- Automatically mirroring them from external sources (such as Google Drive, Salesforce, etc.).
- Directly assigning access to users or groups of users. Often less convenient but requires less
  calls to external APIs which may be preferred in certain deployments.

## Permissions

Permissions are granted to groups, and a user receives the permissions of every group they belong to.

- **Basic**: the default group every user joins. Standard access to chat, search, and personal resources
- **Admin**: the group that grants full system access, audit, and configuration capabilities
- **Custom groups**: grant specific capabilities, such as managing connectors, agents, or query history
  (only available on Enterprise Edition)
- **Group Manager**: a user who manages one group's members and resources, without any
  organization-wide permissions

For the full model, see [Understanding Permissions](/admins/permissions/understanding_permissions).
