The Onyx mobile app is a native app for iOS and Android.
Connect it to your Onyx server and chat with your agents from your phone or tablet.

<Note>
  The Onyx web app is also responsive. If you do not have the mobile app, open your Onyx URL in your mobile browser.
</Note>

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Ask your administrator how your organization distributes the Onyx mobile app for iOS and Android.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your Onyx URL">
    On first launch, enter the URL of your Onyx server, such as `https://onyx.example.com`.
    Onyx Cloud users enter `https://cloud.onyx.app`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your email and password, or with your organization's single sign-on in the browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Features

- Chat with any agent you can access, with streamed answers and citations
- Open a project and chat with its files
- Attach a document or a photo from your library
- Turn on Deep Research or force a specific action for a turn
- Pick the model for a turn
- Stop generation at any time
- Reopen past chats from the sidebar
- Tap a citation to open the sources sheet

## Limitations

The mobile app does not yet support these web app features:

- Edit or regenerate a message
- Give feedback on an answer
- Compare answers from multiple models
- Voice input
- Detailed view of the actions an agent runs

Use the web app for these features.

<Info>
  The mobile app is available on all editions. It uses the same sign-in and permissions as the web app.
</Info>
