## Overview

Spending limits cap how many LLM tokens, or how much LLM cost, Onyx spends in a time period. When a limit is reached,
Onyx rejects new chat messages until the period resets.

<Info>
  **Business Plan Feature**

  Spending limits require a Business plan or higher. Per-user and per-group limits require an Enterprise plan.
  Onyx Cloud includes all of them. [View plans](/admins/billing/overview).
</Info>

To manage limits, open the **Admin Panel**, select **Usage** under **Usage**,
and scroll to **Manage spending limits** at the bottom of the page.

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/advanced_configs/spending_limits.png" alt="Manage spending limits on the Usage page"/>

## Scopes

| Applies to | Who it caps | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Every user, group, and API key together | Business |
| **Per user** | Each user on their own | Enterprise |
| **User group** | All members of one group together | Enterprise |

On an Enterprise plan the table has **Global**, **Users**, and **Groups** tabs.
You can also add limits for one group from its page under **Groups**.

When a user is in several groups, the most permissive group limit applies.
A user is blocked only when every limit that applies to them is reached.

## Create a limit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Create spending limit**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the scope">
    Under **Applies to**, select **Workspace**, **Per user**, or **User group**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the budget">
    Enter a **Cost budget** in USD, a **Token budget** in tokens, or both.
    A limit with both budgets blocks when either one is reached.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the reset period">
    Enter the **Reset period** in days. Budgets reset at midnight UTC.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create limit**. The limit takes effect at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage limits

Each row shows the scope, the budget, the reset period, and a toggle.
Turn the toggle off to pause a limit without deleting it. Use the delete action to remove it.

<Note>
  Spending limits count LLM usage from chat, agents, Craft, the Slack bot, and the API.
  Onyx records usage a few seconds after each response,
  so a user can go slightly over a budget before the block applies.
</Note>
