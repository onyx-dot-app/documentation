## Overview

The Workspace Settings page allows you to configure global settings for your Onyx workspace.

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/advanced_configs/workspace_settings.png" alt="Workspace settings page"/>

### Chat Retention

Setting a Chat Retention policy will delete chat messages after the specified number of days.

<Tip>

  Let your users know what the retention policy is to avoid any surprises!
</Tip>

### Image Processing

Image processing settings are in [Index Settings](/admins/advanced_configs/search_configs#image-processing).
