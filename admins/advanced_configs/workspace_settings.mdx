## Overview

Onyx no longer has a **Workspace Settings** page. The options moved to two pages in the **Admin Panel**.

| Former setting | Current location |
|---|---|
| Chat retention | **Chat Preferences → Advanced Options → Keep Chat History** |
| Image processing and vision model | **Index Settings → Image Processing** |

## Chat Preferences

Open the **Admin Panel** and select **Chat Preferences**. This page holds the workspace defaults for chat:
feature switches, team context, the system prompt, file limits, anonymous access, and chat retention.
See [Chat Preferences](/admins/managing_features/chat) for every option.

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/advanced_configs/workspace_settings.png" alt="Advanced Options on the Chat Preferences page"/>

### Keep Chat History

Under **Advanced Options**, set **Keep Chat History** to **Forever**, a preset (7, 30, 60, or 90 days, or 1 year),
or **Custom Retention** in days. Onyx deletes chats with no activity for longer than the retention period.

<Warning>
  When you reduce retention, Onyx permanently deletes older chats across the workspace. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Enterprise Plan Feature**

  Chat retention requires an Enterprise plan. Onyx Cloud includes it. [View plans](/admins/billing/overview).
</Info>

<Tip>
  Tell your users what the retention policy is to avoid surprises.
</Tip>

## Image Processing

Image extraction and captioning are set on the **Index Settings** page under **Documents & Knowledge**.
Turn on **Extract & Caption Images**, then choose a **Captioning LLM**.
See [Index Settings](/admins/advanced_configs/search_configs#image-processing).
