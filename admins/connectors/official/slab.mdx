## How it works

Slab is a wiki tool where the pages are called posts.
Onyx indexes the **title** and **content** of every post that the bot token can read.

Onyx pulls new and changed posts on the connector's **Refresh Every** schedule. The default is **30 minutes**.

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slab connector">
    In Onyx, go to **Admin Panel → Connectors** and select **Slab**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an authentication account">
    Under **Authentication Account**, select **Create New**. Enter a **Display Name**,
    paste the token into **Slab Bot Token**, and select **Create**. To reuse a saved account,
    select **Use Account** on it instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the connector">
    Under **Connector Settings**, give the connector a **Display Name**. In **Base URL**,
    enter the URL of your Slab team, such as `https://onyx.slab.com/`.
    Onyx uses it to build the link for each indexed post.

    Under **Document Access**, choose who can see the indexed posts.
    **Everyone in Your Organization** shows them to all Onyx users.
    **Specific Groups** limits them to the Onyx user groups you pick. **Specific Groups** is a paid feature:
    the Business and Enterprise tiers on Onyx Cloud, and the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted.
    The Slab connector does not sync Slab permissions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start indexing">
    Select **Create Connector** to save the connector and start indexing.
    Open **Admin Panel → Indexing Status** and select the connector to follow the first indexing run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
